NEON SPACE ARENA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026.

This policy describes data handling in the Neon Space Arena Android game (package identifier: com.amoristik.neonarena).

Developer: Amoristik.
Privacy and support contact: mr.amoristik@gmail.com.

1. Data during gameplay

An internet connection is not required for gameplay. The game has no registration, user accounts, advertising modules, built-in analytics, or built-in automatic crash reporting to the developer.

The game does not request a player's name, email address, phone number, or other contact details; it does not access contacts, location, the camera, the microphone, or photos; it does not use advertising identifiers or send game saves to a developer-operated server.

2. Local saves and settings

The following information is saved in the app's private storage on the device:

  • energy credits, acquired ships and upgrades, and the selected ship;
  • sound effects, music, and vibration settings;
  • the current playthrough: level, health, enemies, projectiles, shield, and battle timers.

This information is used to operate the game and resume a playthrough. Ships, upgrades, and additional hearts are purchased using in-game energy credits; the current version does not accept real-money payments or process payment information.

The game allows Android system backups. Depending on the device settings and the system service, backups may include game saves and settings and may be restored when the app is reinstalled or transferred to another device. Backups are managed by Android or the relevant system service provider; the developer does not have access to these backups.

3. Email enquiries

If you choose to email mr.amoristik@gmail.com, the developer will receive your sender email address, message text, and any attachments you choose to provide. This information is used to respond, resolve the reported issue, and handle privacy requests.

Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information. Gmail is used for email correspondence; its processing and storage of messages are also governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data sharing and system services

The game does not sell user data or share it with advertising or analytics companies. The current version does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or payment SDKs.

Google Play, Android, and device service providers may independently process information related to app distribution, installation, protection, backups, or system diagnostics. Such processing is determined by those services' features, settings, and policies and is not built-in game analytics. See Google's Privacy Policy.

The public webpage for this policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. When the page is visited, GitHub logs the visitor's IP address for security purposes under its Privacy Statement. The page itself has no advertising or analytics scripts or data collection forms and does not set its own cookies.

5. Retention, security, and deletion

Local data is kept in the app's dedicated Android storage, with access restricted by the operating system. Device and backup security also depend on the user's settings and the system services used.

To reset in-game purchases and your playthrough, use the progress reset option in the game's settings; sound effects, music, and vibration preferences are retained. To delete all local data, including settings, clear the app's storage in Android settings. Uninstalling the app also removes its local data, but a system backup may remain separately.

Backups must be deleted through the relevant service's settings. The developer cannot delete them on the user's behalf.

Support correspondence is retained for as long as necessary to handle the enquiry and resolve related matters. To request deletion of correspondence or ask about data handling, email mr.amoristik@gmail.com. Requests will be considered in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

6. Policy changes

If the game's functionality or data handling practices change, this policy will be updated with a new date. If services that process user data are added to the game, the relevant information will be reflected in this policy and the app's store disclosures.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer, Amoristik, at mr.amoristik@gmail.com.
